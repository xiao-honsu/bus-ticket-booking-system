# Help Center

Welcome to the BusTicket Help Center. Here you'll find answers to common questions and guidance on how to use our platform.

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

You can book tickets as a guest or create an account for additional benefits:

- **Guest Booking**: Book tickets without creating an account. You'll receive booking confirmations via email and SMS.
- **Registered Users**: Create an account to track bookings, save preferences, collect promotions, and earn rewards.

To create an account:

1. Click "Sign Up" in the header
2. Enter your name, email, and password
3. Verify your email address
4. Start booking!

### Searching for Trips

1. **Select Route**: Choose your departure and destination cities
2. **Pick a Date**: Select your travel date
3. **Browse Results**: View available trips with departure times, prices, and operators
4. **Compare Options**: Check bus amenities, operator ratings, and available seats

## Booking Process

### Step 1: Select Your Trip

Browse available trips and compare:

- Departure and arrival times
- Ticket prices (may vary by seat type)
- Bus operator and ratings
- Available seats and amenities
- Estimated journey duration

### Step 2: Choose Your Seats

Our interactive seat map shows:

- **Available seats** (green) - Ready to book
- **Locked seats** (yellow) - Temporarily reserved by other users
- **Booked seats** (gray) - Already taken

**Important**: Seats are locked for 10 minutes once selected. Complete your booking within this time to secure your seats.

### Step 3: Enter Passenger Details

Provide information for each passenger:

- Full name (as per ID)
- Age and gender
- Optional: ID document number
- Optional: Phone number

### Step 4: Apply Promotions

- Browse available promotions
- Collect promotions to your account
- Apply promotion codes at checkout
- View discounted prices instantly

### Step 5: Payment

We support multiple payment methods:

- PayOS (recommended)
- Credit/Debit Cards
- Bank Transfer
- E-wallets (MoMo, ZaloPay)

**Payment Security**: All transactions are encrypted and processed through secure payment gateways.

## Managing Bookings

### Viewing Your Bookings

**Registered Users**: Access your bookings from the Dashboard

**Guest Users**: Check booking status using your reference number:

1. Visit "My Bookings"
2. Enter your booking reference number
3. View booking details and status

### Booking Status Explained

- **Pending**: Payment processing or awaiting confirmation
- **Confirmed**: Booking successful, tickets issued
- **Completed**: Journey completed
- **Cancelled**: Booking cancelled (may be eligible for refund)

### Email Notifications

You'll receive email notifications for:

- Booking confirmation
- Payment receipt
- Trip reminders (24 hours and 2 hours before departure)
- Trip updates or changes
- Cancellation confirmations

### Cancellation & Refunds

**Cancellation Policy**:

- Cancellations must be made before the trip's cancellation deadline
- Refund percentage depends on how far in advance you cancel
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method

**How to Cancel**:

1. Go to My Bookings
2. Select the booking to cancel
3. Click "Cancel Booking"
4. Provide reason (optional)
5. Confirm cancellation
6. Refund will be processed within 5-7 business days

**Refund Rates**:

- More than 24 hours before departure: 80-90% refund
- 12-24 hours before: 50% refund
- Less than 12 hours: 20% refund
- After departure: No refund

## Promotions & Discounts

### How Promotions Work

1. **Browse Promotions**: View available promotions on our Promotions page
2. **Collect**: Add promotions to your account (limited per user)
3. **Apply**: Enter promotion code at checkout
4. **Save**: Discount applied automatically

### Promotion Types

- **Fixed Amount**: Deduct a specific amount from your booking
- **Percentage**: Get a percentage off (with maximum discount limit)
- **Minimum Order**: Some promotions require minimum booking value

### Promotion Terms

- Each promotion has usage limits
- Promotions are valid between specific dates
- One promotion per booking
- Cannot be combined with other offers
- Some promotions are user-specific
